PRIVACY POLICY

How TallyTime handles your data

Version 2026-10-12. Written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

In short

  • Your documents are deleted after 7 days, with their results, reviews and audit log. You can delete them sooner at any time.
  • No outside AI service sees your documents. The AI model (IBM Granite) runs on our own server.
  • Only your workspace can see your shipments. Nobody else using TallyTime can.
  • We do not sell data, show ads or use tracking or analytics cookies.

Who we are

TallyTime is run by Akash A, Kerala, India ("we"). We decide how the data described here is used, which makes us the Data Fiduciary under the DPDP Act. Contact: privacy@akashanil.dev.

Cookies

We use one cookie, tt_session, to keep you signed in. It is essential, so there is no cookie banner. Your browser may also remember small settings such as your review team's names; these stay on your device.

Who else handles data for us

  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure hosts the server where everything is stored and processed.
  • Brevo delivers our emails (confirmation, password reset, invitations and, if you turn them on, "check finished" emails). It receives your email address and the email's text, which never includes document contents.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that sign-ups and password resets come from a person, not a bot.
  • If you turn on notifications, your browser's push service (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple) delivers them. The messages are encrypted so the push service cannot read them.

We share data with no one else, unless the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct or update it (your name can be changed in Account settings);
  • erase it: delete any shipment at any time, or delete your whole account in Account settings. Deletion is immediate;
  • withdraw consent, which you do by deleting your account;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated;
  • complain to us, and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, or to raise a grievance, email privacy@akashanil.dev. We will reply as soon as we can, and in any case within the time the law requires.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed with Argon2, and sign-in links are single-use and expire. Every request checks that you belong to the shipment's workspace. Nightly backups hold only account details, never documents or results. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Children

TallyTime is a business tool for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the version date above and ask you to accept the new version the next time you sign in.
